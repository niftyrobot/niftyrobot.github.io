Wordy Bird

Privacy Policy

Effective June 6, 2026

Wordy Bird (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a word puzzle game published by Nifty Robot. This policy describes what information the app collects and how it is used. We’ve kept it plain-spoken on purpose — there isn’t much to it.

Information we collect

Wordy Bird does not require an account, and we do not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The app uses only the data it needs to run:

  • On your device. Your scores, badges, streaks, settings, and game progress are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.
  • Google AdMob serves the ads inside the app. AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers, IP address, general (city or country level) location, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google’s privacy policy for details.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play process in-app purchases. We do not see or store your payment information — the store handles it. We receive a confirmation that a purchase has been made so the corresponding feature can be unlocked.
  • Anonymous leaderboard data. If you participate in the global leaderboard, the app sends an anonymous identifier and your score so your ranking can be shown. No personally identifying information is included.

How we use information

The information described above is used only to:

  • Run the game on your device.
  • Show ads, until you choose to remove them via in-app purchase.
  • Process and verify purchases through the App Store or Google Play.
  • Display your position on the global leaderboard, if you use it.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising other than through the AdMob service described above.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking. On iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Remove ads. Purchase Remove Ads inside the app to stop seeing advertisements.
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children’s privacy

Wordy Bird is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
